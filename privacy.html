Privacy Policy for Bible Picture Puzzle

Last Updated: October 30, 2026 • Published by Sujy Games

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Sujy Games ("we", "us", or "our") regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of your information when you use our mobile application, Bible Picture Puzzle (the "Service"). It also informs you about your privacy rights and how applicable laws protect you.

We use your data to provide, maintain, and improve the Service. By installing and using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms outlined herein, please do not install or use the Service.

Data Collection and Processing

We collect limited information to provide, optimize, and secure your puzzle gameplay experience. All data transmitted by the Service is securely encrypted in transit using HTTPS (SSL/TLS) protocols.

Security Guarantee: We do not sell your personal data. All gameplay progress is linked to anonymous identifiers, and no account registration or social login is required to play.

A. Location Data

  • Data Type: Coarse / approximate location derived from IP address (country or regional level only). We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Collection Type: Automatically collected and processed by third-party SDKs.
  • Purposes:
    • Analytics: Used by Firebase Analytics to determine aggregated player demographics by country.
    • Advertising: Used by ad mediation networks (such as Unity LevelPlay and Google AdMob) to serve regional, localized advertisements.

B. Personal Data & Account Information

  • No Account Required: Bible Picture Puzzle does not require user registration, login, or creation of accounts with passwords or social credentials.
  • Installation & Device Identifiers: We automatically generate or collect anonymous installation identifiers (e.g., Firebase Installation ID). This is required to maintain game progression (completed puzzle books, levels unlocked), ensure security, and prevent fraud across ad and reward mechanisms.

C. Financial Information & Purchases (In-App Purchases)

  • Data Type: Transaction receipts and purchase state tokens processed through the Google Play Billing system.
  • Purposes: To verify transactions and grant digital puzzle items, hints, or "no-ads" privileges, and prevent fraudulent unlock attempts.
  • Note: We do not collect or store credit card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses; all financial payments are handled exclusively and securely by Google Play.

D. App Activity and Gameplay Interactions

  • Gameplay Progress: We collect data regarding your in-game activity, such as puzzle levels started, completed, failed, or restarted, book unlocks (Book 1 to Book 10), duration to assemble pictures, and hint usage.
  • Preferences & UI Actions: We store user audio preferences (music and sound toggles) and haptic feedback settings. We track ad-interaction events (such as rewarded ad views for hints) to accurately dispense rewards.

E. App Performance, Diagnostics, and Crash Reports

  • Crash Logs: We collect stack traces and error logs via Firebase Crashlytics when an application crash or unexpected exception occurs.
  • Diagnostics: We gather device telemetry (device brand, device model, operating system version, and initialization latency) to diagnose technical bugs, resolve crashes, and ensure smooth performance across various Android devices.

Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers that assist in app delivery, performance monitoring, and advertising mediation:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: Provides aggregated usage metrics to understand gameplay difficulty and improve puzzle designs.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Receives automated crash reports and hardware specifications to diagnose and fix technical defects.
  • Ad Mediation & Networks (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Google AdMob, Unity Ads): Receives device identifiers and coarse location to serve advertisements, manage ad frequency, and measure ad performance.
  • Meta / Facebook SDK: Used solely for measuring app installation and basic engagement events.
  • Legal Compliance & Protection: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law or in response to valid requests by public authorities to protect the safety, rights, or property of our users or the Company.

Google Play Families Policy & Children’s Privacy

Bible Picture Puzzle is designed as a family-friendly biblical puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages, including children and families:

  • Families Policy Compliance: We comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • No Personal Data Collection from Children: We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personally identifiable information (such as real names, home addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses) from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
  • Family-Safe Advertisements: Any advertisements served within the Application to child audiences or general family audiences are handled strictly through certified ad networks that comply with Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK requirements. Ad networks are instructed not to serve personalized (interest-based) behavioral ads to children and not to collect the Google Advertising ID for interest-based profiling.
  • Parental Rights & Inquiries: If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, or if you wish to review or delete any information associated with your child’s device, please contact us immediately at sujygames@gmail.com, and we will promptly take steps to delete such data.

Data Retention, Security, and Transfer

Retention: We retain gameplay analytics, crash telemetry, and installation identifiers only as long as necessary to provide game functionality, maintain security, and fulfill legal compliance requirements. Anonymous crash and analytics data are periodically purged in accordance with Google Firebase's standard retention cycles.

Security: The security of your data is paramount. All data transmitted between the Application and third-party servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (SSL/TLS). While we use commercially acceptable means to protect information, please be aware that no electronic transmission or digital storage method is 100% secure.

International Transfers: Information processed by our third-party service providers (such as Google and Unity) may be stored and processed on servers located outside of your home country or jurisdiction. These providers adhere to applicable cross-border data transfer safeguards.

Data Deletion & User Rights

You have the right to request the deletion or correction of any data collected about you.

  • How to Request: Because Bible Picture Puzzle does not use accounts or logins, data is associated with your anonymous installation or device identifier. You can reset your identifier by clearing the app data in your device settings, or you can contact us directly at sujygames@gmail.com to request data deletion. We will process your request promptly in accordance with applicable privacy laws.
